## 2. Executive 2-Minute Summary & Headline Results

This independent capstone carries 10 listed Latin American Industrials and Real Estate entities through all six course blocks to deliver an actionable portfolio capital-allocation decision for the \$250M *Latin America Sustainable Opportunities Equity & Debt Fund*:

1. **Foundations & Transmission (Block A):** Classifies 10 holdings into primary physical flood peril versus transition carbon pricing channels. High-margin logistics REITs pass through carbon compliance costs via triple-net (NNN) leases, whereas heavy freight transport operators face direct OpEx erosion.
2. **Data Governance & Portfolio Carbon (Block B):** Audits emissions disclosure across the universe (100.0% Scope 1/2 coverage, 70.0% Scope 3). Portfolio WACI is reduced by **21.6%** (from 191.5 to **150.1 tCO2 / MUSD revenue**), exceeding the fund's 20% decarbonization mandate.
3. **Transition DCF Valuation (Block A–B):** A 10-year discounted cash flow model under NGFS Phase IV pathways reveals that FIBRA Prologis enterprise value remains virtually immune to carbon pricing (**<0.01% EV contraction** under \$120/tCO2 Orderly Transition), whereas Rumo S.A. suffers a catastrophic **16.4% to 31.2% EV loss** under unhedged diesel compliance costs.
4. **ESG Divergence & Stewardship (Block C):** Reconciles disclosure transparency against controversy-adjusted governance (mean divergence: 0.75 pts). Verifies FIBRA Prologis green building claims via Sustainalytics Second-Party Opinion (SPO), while identifying major reporting gaps in Rumo S.A. Formulates a concrete 12-month board stewardship mandate.
5. **Focal Asset Physical Flood Risk Transfer (Block D):** 20,000 Monte Carlo draws on the \$120M Santos megahub show an unmitigated Expected Annual Loss (EAL) of **\$3.28M** and a P95 tail loss of **\$33.15M**. A layered resilience strategy (structural adaptation + parametric excess-of-loss insurance) collapses P95 tail loss by **87.9% to \$4.00M**, delivering a positive 20-year NPV of **+\$18.4M** with minimized basis risk.
6. **Focal Green Bond Financials & Policy (Block E):** The USD 500M 5.250% Use-of-Proceeds Green Bond commands an **8.5 bps greenium**, yielding \$0.500 NPV savings per \$100 face value. Covenants include a +25 bps penalty step-up coupon for reporting breaches. Aligns with Mexican and Colombian Green Taxonomies and directly mirrors **SEBI's Regulatory Framework for ESG Debt Securities in India (SEBI Operational Circular 2023)** and the Indian Sovereign Green Bond Framework.
7. **Synthesis & Integration (Block F):** Outputs are synthesized via the Composite Climate-Adjusted Value & Resilience Score (CAVRS), generating an optimized portfolio allocation that boosts resilience (+0.045 CAVRS) and deconcentrates carbon risk.
